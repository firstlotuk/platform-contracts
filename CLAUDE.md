
## Platform versioning

This module owns its **own** semver (its `package.json` version and/or its own
`vX.Y.Z` tags). The **overall FirstLot platform version** (`firstlot/<X.Y.Z>`), the
release ledger, and the "how to start new work" convention live in **firstlot-specs
→ `specs/RELEASES.md`** (workstreams: `0.5.x` platform/auth/security/BFF, `0.6.x`
rule-engine/triage). Do not stamp platform version numbers onto this repo.
